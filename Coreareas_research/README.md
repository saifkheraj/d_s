# Core Areas of Research

Three pillars of applied mathematics and decision science.

---

## 1_OperationsResearch/

Optimization, decision-making under constraints, and analytical modeling.

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **Theory/** | LP fundamentals — Simplex, Duality, Sensitivity Analysis, MCNF (network flows, shortest path, max flow, transportation) |
| **DiscreteOptimization/** | Integer programming, combinatorial optimization |
| **FacilityLocation/** | Facility location problem — notebook + input data (CSVs) |
| **OperationsManagement/** | Operations management concepts |
| **MMM/** | Marketing Mix Modeling |

## 2_RL/

Reinforcement Learning — sequential decision-making and stochastic processes.

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **1_Sequential_Decision_Making/** | Foundations of sequential decisions under uncertainty |
| **2_Markovchains/** | Markov chains and stochastic processes |

## 3_Graph/

Graph theory and graph-based machine learning.

| Folder | Contents |
|--------|----------|
| **Embeddings/** | Graph embeddings, random walk methods, neural network approaches, spectral methods (Laplacian, eigenvectors) |
